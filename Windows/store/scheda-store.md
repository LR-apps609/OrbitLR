## English

**Name:** OrbitLR

**Short description**
Drop a file, pick a slice, done. Convert, compress and fix photos, videos, audio and PDFs on your computer, without uploading anything.

**Description**
Upload a file. Pick a slice. Done.

OrbitLR puts a pie around your file with everything you can do with it: convert it, make it lighter, trim it, fix it. One click and the new file sits next to the original.

Everything happens on your computer: your photos, videos and documents are never uploaded. No account, no ads, no subscriptions.

PHOTOS
• Convert to JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, PDF and more
• Compress under an exact size (e.g. 200 KB for an online form)
• Remove backgrounds and logos with on-device AI
• Enhance and upscale, straighten a photographed document, extract text
• Remove GPS location and other metadata

VIDEO
• Compress for WhatsApp, Discord or email while keeping the quality
• Trim, edit with titles, filters and music, automatic subtitles
• 9:16 social format for Reels and TikTok, with framing that follows people
• Remove silences, extract audio, download a video from a link

AUDIO
• Convert to MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC
• Make a ringtone, clean up voice noise, transcribe to text

PDF & DOCUMENTS
• Compress, merge, split, rotate, reorder
• Fill in, sign and redact, add or remove a password
• Convert Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF

Plus: watch folders that tidy up incoming files on their own, "My actions" for choices you use often, Ctrl+Alt+C from File Explorer, sending a file to your phone with a QR code on the same Wi-Fi, light and dark theme, English and Italian.
