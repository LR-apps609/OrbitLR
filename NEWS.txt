What’s new in 5.0.1 “Tools”:
• Editor: in the Clip tab the “Trim” part is now “Tools”, and the clip speed (0.25× to 4×) is there too, as well as in Effects
• Permissions at first launch: notifications no longer wait forever. OrbitLR tells you where to look (the notice at the top right) and, if you don’t answer, moves on: you can allow them later. “Later” works while it asks, too

What’s new in 5.0 “Simple”:
• The new OrbitLR: every section (Images, Video, Audio, PDFs and documents) opens in “Simple”, with the 6 things people do most, big. At the top the “Simple · Complete” switch: Complete has every feature, in groups that open one at a time. Each section remembers your choice
• In Video “Editor” and in PDFs and documents “PDF editor” are in the same switch
• Clearer names: “Convert”, “PDFs and documents”, “Watch Folders”
• Vector with clean shapes: straight sides, regular stars and polygons, edges aligned across shapes, exact when the drawing was
• Switching between sections is faster

What’s new in 4.6 “Pack”:
• Vector (SVG) → “Logo pack (ZIP)”: from the same logo, every file you need. SVG for the web, vector PDF and EPS for print shops, transparent PNGs at 512, 1024 and 2048 pixels
• The one-color version, black and white, the way a designer makes it: areas alternate filled and empty, so stars, circles and lettering stay visible. For stamps, engraving and dark backgrounds
• If the logo is almost square, icons for a website too (favicon and iPhone icon). A README inside explains which file to use

What’s new in 4.5.1 “Percentage”:
• Updates with a percentage: the button at the top shows “Downloading the update… 86%” while it downloads, like on Windows

What’s new in 4.5 “Extreme”:
• Vector (SVG) → “Extreme”: after tracing, OrbitLR draws the whole SVG, compares it with the original pixel by pixel and moves all the points of all the curves, and the colors, together until it stops improving
• Signatures and line drawings: it tries several different tracings, one per core, and keeps the one that matches best; the ink color is now the true one
• Merged gradients: where a color changes slowly there are no more bands, just one gradient
• It uses all cores but one, at low priority: the Mac stays usable. Up to 5 minutes, usually less

What’s new in 4.4.1 “Centered”:
• The “New update” button at the top is centered like the other toolbar buttons

What’s new in 4.4 “Tracing”:
• Vector (SVG) → “Maximum precision”: OrbitLR traces every curve onto the true edge of the image, point by point, as if by hand. From the soft pixels along the edges it works out where the outline runs, finer than a pixel
• Sharp corners where two straight sides meet, curves that run exactly on the edge, colors taken from inside each shape
• It is slower (up to a few minutes on large images) but gives the most faithful result: for logos, text, icons and signatures

What’s new in 4.3 “Vector”:
• Images → “Vector (SVG)”, also as an SVG slice in the pie: logos, drawings, signatures and photos become an SVG that stays sharp at any size
• Clean colors without halos, straight lines, crisp corners, true circles and gradients where they belong; on small images the edges are first rebuilt with AI, on your Mac
• Signatures and scans in a single color with a transparent background; “Logo without background” removes the background by itself
• The same engine as OrbitLR for Windows: the same file gives the same SVG on both

What’s new in 4.2.1 “English Names”:
• The files you create now have English names too: “photo (smaller).jpg”, “song (trimmed).mp3”, “video (with music).mp4”

What’s new in 4.2 “English”:
• OrbitLR now speaks English: it follows your Mac’s language, or you can choose it in Settings → Appearance → “Language · Lingua” (OrbitLR reopens by itself)
• Everything is in English, including engine messages, numbers with a decimal point, History months, and the “Converted” and “To Compress” folders
